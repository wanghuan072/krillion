# Krillion — The Daily Dive

[Krillion](https://krilliongames.com/) is a daily open-answer trivia game built around a simple challenge: give a correct answer that fewer players are likely to choose. Every Daily Dive contains seven prompts, and each prompt gives you 25 seconds to read the category, recall a few valid possibilities, and submit one answer.

Correctness comes first, but rarity determines how far the answer takes you. Familiar responses are dependable and often score modestly. Less obvious answers can reach deeper tiers when they still match every word of the prompt. The final score from all seven questions becomes your dive depth, turning a short trivia run into a test of knowledge, recall, judgment, and composure.

## What makes Krillion different?

### Seven shared daily prompts

Players receive the same set of seven open-ended categories for the day. A run may move between geography, entertainment, history, sport, science, food, brands, language, and general knowledge. Breadth is often more useful than expertise in only one subject.

### Rarity matters, but validity matters more

An obscure answer cannot help if it belongs to the wrong category, misses a required letter, falls outside a stated date range, or uses a name the game cannot recognize. The strongest answer is usually the least obvious option that remains clearly valid and conventionally written.

### Every answer affects the final depth

Accepted answers fall into six visible rarity tiers: **Plankton**, **Too Clever**, **Schooler**, **Rare**, **Deep Cut**, and **One in a Krillion**. A complete run of sensible answers usually performs better than one spectacular answer surrounded by timeouts or invalid guesses.

### Short decisions create real tension

Twenty-five seconds is enough time to think, but not enough time to research or debate every edge case. Krillion rewards a repeatable routine: understand the prompt, build a short list, remove risky candidates, type the standard name, and submit before the clock becomes the main problem.

## A practical way to play

1. Read the complete prompt before typing. Identify the requested type and every qualifier.
2. Let the first answer in your mind become the safe option.
3. Think of one or two alternatives that are less automatic but still clearly legal.
4. Reject anything that needs a long explanation to prove it belongs.
5. Use the shortest standard spelling rather than a nickname or descriptive phrase.
6. Treat five seconds remaining as the point to submit or switch to the safe answer.
7. Read the rarity result, remember one lesson, and reset your attention for the next prompt.

This approach does not guarantee a perfect score, but it prevents the most common failures: category mistakes, avoidable spelling problems, overthinking, and empty submissions.

## Tips for a deeper Daily Dive

- Choose validity before rarity. A common accepted answer always beats a clever category error.
- Move one step away from the obvious answer instead of searching for the most obscure fact you know.
- Pay close attention to words such as *former*, *current*, *before*, *above*, *fictional*, and *English name*.
- Keep a safe backup ready whenever your preferred answer has uncertain spelling or category status.
- Do not add explanations to the answer field. Use the ordinary name a reference source would recognize.
- On mobile, submit longer proper nouns earlier and watch for autocorrect changing uncommon names.
- Review the route that produced a strong answer, not only the answer itself. “Former teams” or “supporting characters” is more reusable than memorizing one isolated result.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many questions are in a Krillion Daily Dive?

Every run contains seven prompts. You have 25 seconds to answer each one.

### How does Krillion scoring work?

An accepted answer receives a rarity tier worth 10, 15, 30, 60, 85, or 100 points. The total from all seven prompts is multiplied by ten to produce the displayed depth in metres.

### What is the highest possible result?

Seven **One in a Krillion** answers would produce 700 points and a depth of 7,000 metres. That is a theoretical maximum; attempting an extremely risky answer on every prompt is rarely the most reliable strategy.

### Should I always choose the rarest answer I can think of?

No. Choose the rarest answer that you can confidently classify, spell, and submit in time. If an answer depends on a technicality or a long argument, the safer candidate is usually better.

### Can I submit another answer after a rejection?

The game may leave the timer running and allow another attempt. Prepare a simple backup before submitting a risky answer so you can respond immediately if the first choice is not accepted.

### Why did an answer I considered clever receive a common tier?

Personal difficulty and overall rarity are not the same thing. Other players may reach the same answer quickly, especially when it is the first recognizable alternative to the obvious response.

### Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Krillion works in a modern mobile browser, although a physical keyboard leaves more time for recall. On touch devices, avoid rotating the screen during a prompt and check that autocorrect has not changed a proper noun before submitting.

### What should I do if the game does not load?

Use the visible retry control once, then check your connection and browser privacy settings. Content filters or temporary availability problems can prevent an external game player from opening. The [loading and input guide](https://krilliongames.com/guides/krillion-loading-and-input-help) provides a complete recovery checklist.

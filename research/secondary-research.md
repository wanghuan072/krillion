# Secondary Research and Content Decisions

Date: 2026-09-21  
Public language and market: English, global browser players

## Method

The second pass compared provider catalog records, controlled non-provider-origin launches, visible live interfaces, and candidate gameplay-video identifiers. Aggregator pages were used only to locate candidates or confirm title/version alignment. Their prose, images, page structure, and comments were not reused. All public articles were written around the player's actual task and the current visible controls.

## Krillion

The supplied daily address was preserved exactly. The visible game presents seven timed typed prompts and a descent result, but the iframe did not complete a bounded local-origin interactive run. Public copy therefore explains the observable flow without claiming an immutable scoring formula or verified embedded play. The player must fail safely when the URL cannot be used. A title-specific, currently embeddable YouTube video has not yet been confirmed; this remains the one media issue that can block publication in Flow 08.

## Additional games

All eight selected games use direct versioned Famobi CDN builds rather than the `play.famobi.com` launcher page, because the launcher opens a new tab instead of providing in-frame play. Guess Their Answer was advanced beyond its consent and launch screens to the avatar/name/play interface on a local non-provider origin. The remaining builds showed their own title-specific playable surfaces and will receive full interaction checks and screenshots during the media pass.

Content decisions are title-specific: Guess Their Answer emphasizes popular survey answers; Words of Wonders uses crossword crossings; Word Detector uses word-length inventory; Word Search Classic uses straight-line scanning; Text Twist 2 prioritizes the full-rack bingo; Animal Quiz combines anatomy and letter constraints; Sweet Hangman manages limited misses; Word Bird treats the board as a neighboring-tile route puzzle.

## Content quality checks

Each game article has a distinct explanation of the start flow, controls, objective, progress, common mistakes, first-play strategy, device issues, and verified questions. All nine exceed the 4,000 non-whitespace visible-character floor before videos. The three Guide articles live in separate JSON files and share their data with routes, metadata, related links, structured data, and the future sitemap.
